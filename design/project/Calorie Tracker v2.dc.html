Calorie tracker · MVP

Live prototype · v2 warm

Galaxy A53 viewport, 412 × 915. Everything is wired: days, meals, library, week and settings share one local state. Try the main flow: Add → Camera → note → Recognize → Confirm & save. Recognize returns a clean result, a low-confidence estimate or a failure at random, so run it a few times.

Today
Arrows or tap the date for a 7-day strip. Tap an item to edit or delete it.
Add
Photos are tagged plate / label and can be toggled. The mic fakes a transcript into the note.
Review
Change an amount and the macros rescale. “Add a note & re-run” with a number (e.g. 200) corrects the estimated item.
Library
Tap a product to add it to a meal in grams or portions; star marks favourites. Saved review items land here.
Assumptions: the live prototype opens on Sign in (Google or email link, or skip). Dates fixed to Thu 1 Oct 2026 for the demo. Long-press delete is modelled as Delete inside the item sheet (long-press has no desktop equivalent). Goal and amount units are editable in Settings; the Tweaks panel also exposes the starting screen and scenario.

Sage for the primary action and “today”; a blush background and three soft pastels (rose, peach, sage) for the macro cards and hints. Selected chips use ink, so nothing competes with the accent. Amber is reserved for “estimated”, quiet enough to not read as an error.
